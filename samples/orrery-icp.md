# Orrery Revenue Software: who we sell to

## The short version

Orrery sells a revenue operations platform for B2B sales teams: pipeline, forecasting and territory planning. It is not a payments product. We do best with mid-sized companies that sell to other businesses and run a real sales team.

## Size

A company with 200 to 5,000 employees is the right size. Below 200 there is rarely a sales team big enough to need a platform. Above 5,000 the purchase becomes a procurement project and the deal takes a year.

## Where we win

Software, logistics technology and payments companies are our strongest verticals. Their sales teams are built around pipeline and forecast, which is exactly what we sell.

Agencies rarely close, because their revenue is billable hours and not pipeline. Consultancies rarely close for the same reason. Companies that sell only to consumers are never a fit.

## How we tier a company

- Tier 1: a company that sells to other businesses, sits in one of the strong verticals, and has 200 to 5,000 employees.
- Tier 2: any other company that sells to other businesses and either has 200 to 5,000 employees or sits in a strong vertical. A 30,000-person payments company is tier 2, and so is a 700-person manufacturer.
- Tier 3: agencies, consultancies, companies that sell only to consumers, and everything else.

## Who we talk to

The buyer is whoever owns the revenue number: VP Sales, Head of Revenue Operations and Chief Revenue Officer. Directors and managers in sales can open the door but rarely sign. Marketing and customer success leaders influence the decision. Assistants, interns and coordinators are never the contact.

A VP at a bank or an insurer carries less weight than a VP at a software company, because financial firms have many VPs. A founder at a company under 50 people is the buyer whatever the title says.

## Kinds of company we see

Software vendor, payments provider, logistics technology provider, agency, consultancy, financial institution, manufacturer or distributor, consumer brand.

## Who we skip

Rivalcrest Systems is a direct competitor and never gets contacted. Pinefold is already a customer, and the account team owns that relationship.
